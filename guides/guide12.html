☁️ Secure Cloud Storage

Store and share files with safer security practices.

Why it matters

Cloud storage like Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive or Dropbox is convenient for backups and sharing, but it also holds copies of your photos, documents and IDs on someone else's servers. A hacked account or a careless share link can expose everything.

Steps

  1. Secure the account first. Use a unique, strong password from your password manager and turn on two-factor authentication.
  2. Know what is stored. Look through your files and remove or move sensitive ones such as scans of IDs, passport and bank documents.
  3. Encrypt the most sensitive files before uploading with a tool such as Cryptomator or a zip file with a strong password, or use a provider that offers end-to-end (zero-knowledge) encryption.
  4. Share carefully. Share with specific people instead of "anyone with the link", choose view-only when possible, and set an expiry date.
  5. Review who has access every few months, and remove old shares and apps that are connected to your cloud account.
  6. Keep a second backup on an external drive or another service. A good rule is 3 copies, on 2 types of storage, with 1 copy kept offsite.
  7. Check your login history and sign out of devices you no longer use.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Do not store lists of passwords in a normal cloud document.
  • Remember that deleting a file may not remove it right away, because many services keep deleted items in a trash folder for a while.
