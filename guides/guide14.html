♻️ Discarding Old Tech Safely

Prepare devices before recycling, selling, or donating them.

Why it matters

Old phones, laptops, tablets and hard drives still hold photos, saved passwords, messages and documents, even if you think you deleted them. Before you sell, donate, recycle or throw one away, you must wipe it properly.

Steps

  1. Back up what you want to keep to a new device, drive or cloud account, and check that the backup works.
  2. Sign out of your accounts and turn off tracking and lock features such as Find My (Apple) or Find My Device and factory reset protection (Android).
  3. Remove the SIM card and any memory card.
  4. Make sure the device is encrypted. Modern iPhones and most newer Android phones encrypt storage by default. On computers, turn on BitLocker (Windows) or FileVault (Mac) before wiping.
  5. Do a factory reset. On phones, use "Erase All Content and Settings" (iPhone) or "Factory data reset" (Android). On computers, use the "Reset this PC, remove everything" option or macOS Erase Assistant.
  6. Check that it is clean. The device should restart at the welcome or setup screen.
  7. Remove the old device from the device list in your Apple, Google and other important accounts.
  8. For old hard drives or sensitive data, use a proper erase tool or physically destroy the drive. Take what is left to a certified e-waste recycler.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Simply deleting files or emptying the recycle bin does not remove the data.
  • Do not forget other devices with personal data: routers, smart speakers, game consoles, smart TVs, USB drives and SD cards.
