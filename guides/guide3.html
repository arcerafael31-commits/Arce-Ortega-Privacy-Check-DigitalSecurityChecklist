🌐 Secure Browser Settings

Adjust browser settings to reduce tracking and unsafe activity.

Why it matters

Your browser is the window to almost everything you do online. Advertisers and trackers use cookies, scripts and browser "fingerprints" to follow you from site to site. A few settings and one or two extensions greatly reduce this.

Steps

  1. Keep the browser updated. Turn on automatic updates, because updates fix security holes.
  2. Block third-party cookies and turn on tracking protection. Look in Settings under Privacy and security (the name varies by browser).
  3. Turn on HTTPS-only mode so the browser avoids unencrypted websites.
  4. Review site permissions. Check which sites can use your location, camera, microphone and notifications, and remove the ones you do not need.
  5. Add a small number of trusted extensions, such as an ad and tracker blocker. EFF's Privacy Badger is one example. More extensions means more risk, so remove ones you do not use.
  6. Choose a private search engine and turn off personalized ads in your browser and Google or Apple account settings.
  7. Clear cookies and site data now and then, or set the browser to clear them when it closes.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Private or incognito mode only hides your history from other people using your device. It does not hide you from websites or your internet provider.
  • Download extensions only from the official store and check who made them.
  • Browser fingerprinting identifies you by your device and settings, not cookies, so blocking cookies alone does not stop it.
