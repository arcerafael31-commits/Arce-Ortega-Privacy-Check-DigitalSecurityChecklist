👥 Social Media Privacy Settings

Control who can see your posts and personal details.

Why it matters

Social media profiles are a gold mine for scammers, stalkers and data brokers. Birthdays, schools, pets' names, locations and photos can be used to guess passwords, answer security questions or plan scams. The default settings usually favor sharing.

Steps

  1. Run each platform's privacy checkup. Most platforms have one in Settings under Privacy or Safety.
  2. Make your account private or limit posts to friends and followers you know.
  3. Check who can find you by phone number or email address, and limit search engines from linking to your profile if the option exists.
  4. Review old posts and tags. Limit the audience of past posts and turn on approval for tags.
  5. Turn off location sharing in posts and in the app's phone permissions.
  6. Review connected apps and "Log in with" connections and remove the ones you do not use.
  7. Turn on 2FA and check the list of devices logged in to your account.
  8. Clean up your friends list and delete accounts you no longer use.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Think before posting boarding passes, ID cards, your home, your school or work, or photos that show your address.
  • Avoid the quiz posts that ask for your first car, street or pet's name. They match common security questions.
  • Settings change often. Repeat the checkup every few months.
