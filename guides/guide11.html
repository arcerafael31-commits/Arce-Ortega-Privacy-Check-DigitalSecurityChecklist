💬 Encrypted Messaging Apps

Choose messaging tools with strong privacy protections.

Why it matters

Normal text messages (SMS) and some chat apps can be read by the company running them or by someone who intercepts them. With end-to-end encryption, only you and the person you message can read the content, not even the app's company.

Steps

  1. Know what to look for: end-to-end encryption turned on by default, well-reviewed security, and as little data collection as possible.
  2. Pick an app your contacts will actually use. Signal is widely recommended for privacy. WhatsApp and iMessage also use end-to-end encryption for messages. Telegram only does it in "Secret Chats", not in normal chats.
  3. Install from the official app store and keep the app updated.
  4. Turn on extra protection: a registration lock or PIN, and a screen lock for the app.
  5. Use disappearing messages for sensitive conversations.
  6. For very important contacts, verify the safety number or security code in person or by another channel, so you know nobody is in the middle.
  7. Check the backups. Chat backups to the cloud may not be encrypted unless you turn that on (WhatsApp offers encrypted backups).
  8. Hide message previews on your lock screen.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Encryption protects the message, but not the people. Someone can still screenshot, forward or see it on your unlocked phone.
  • Even with encryption, some apps collect data about who you talk to and when (metadata).
