📱 Mobile Phone Privacy (iOS)

Review privacy and security settings on iPhone devices.

Why it matters

Your iPhone holds your messages, photos, location history and payment cards. Apple gives you strong controls, but several are off or set loosely by default. This guide covers the main ones. Menu names can change a little between iOS versions.

Steps

  1. Update iOS in Settings, General, Software Update, and turn on automatic updates.
  2. Set a strong passcode. Use a longer numeric or alphanumeric code, and turn on Face ID or Touch ID.
  3. Review app permissions. Open Settings, Privacy & Security, and go through Location Services, Photos, Contacts, Microphone and Camera. Set apps to "While Using" or "Never" unless they truly need more.
  4. Limit tracking. In Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking, turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
  5. Turn on Find My iPhone (Settings, your name, Find My) so you can locate or erase a lost phone.
  6. Secure your Apple Account with two-factor authentication and review the trusted devices listed there.
  7. Run Safety Check (iOS 16 or later) in Settings, Privacy & Security, to review who and what has access to your information.
  8. Consider Lockdown Mode only if you are at high risk of targeted attacks, because it limits some features.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Install apps only from the App Store and delete ones you no longer use.
  • Turn off lock screen access to notifications, Wallet and control features if others might pick up your phone.
