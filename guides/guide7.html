🤖 Mobile Phone Privacy (Android)

Protect personal information and permissions on Android.

Why it matters

Android phones can store a huge amount of personal data, and apps often ask for more access than they need. Android has tools to see exactly what each app is using. Menu names differ between phone brands and Android versions, so use the search box in Settings if you cannot find something.

Steps

  1. Update Android and your apps. Install system and security updates as soon as they are offered.
  2. Set a strong screen lock (a longer PIN or password) plus fingerprint or face unlock.
  3. Open the Privacy dashboard. Go to Settings, Security and privacy (or Privacy), Privacy dashboard. It shows which apps used your location, camera and microphone (the last 7 days on Android 13 and newer).
  4. Change app permissions. Tap a permission, then an app, and choose "Allow only while using the app" or "Don't allow" for anything that does not need it.
  5. Remove apps you do not use and install apps only from Google Play or sources you trust.
  6. Keep Google Play Protect on so apps are scanned for harmful behavior.
  7. Run the Google Account Security Checkup and turn on 2-step verification.
  8. Turn on Find My Device so you can locate, lock or erase a lost phone.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Be careful with permissions such as Accessibility, Display over other apps and Install unknown apps. Scam apps abuse them.
  • Turn off personalized ads in your Google account's ad settings if you do not want your data used for ad targeting.
